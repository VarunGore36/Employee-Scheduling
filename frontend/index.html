Duty roster desk

asking the engine…

Period

A roster covers one month at a time, starting on whichever date the office needs — not necessarily the 1st.

Staff

Everyone who can be rostered, and the roles each of them covers. One person may hold more than one role.

Shifts

The shifts in a day, how long each one runs, and how many people each role needs on it.

Rules

Hard rules must hold for a roster to be legal. Soft rules are preferences and fairness goals, traded against each other by weight. Add, edit or drop them here — the engine is asked to accept every change before it is kept.

Check

Before anyone waits on a search: is this month even possible? The engine weighs the demand against the staff who are qualified and free.

Roster

Every duty covered first, then the rest of the time spent trading the soft rules against each other. What the engine gave up is listed underneath, rule by rule.
