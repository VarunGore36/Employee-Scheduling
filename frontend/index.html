Duty roster desk

asking the engine…

Period

A roster covers one month at a time, starting on whichever date the office needs — not necessarily the 1st.

Staff

Everyone who can be rostered, and the roles each of them covers. One person may hold more than one role.

Shifts

The shifts in a day, how long each one runs, and how many people each role needs on it.

Rules

Hard rules must hold for a roster to be legal. Soft rules are preferences and fairness goals, traded against each other by weight.

Check

Before anyone waits on a search: is this month even possible? The engine weighs the demand against the staff who are qualified and free.
